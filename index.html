ASL 1 • Unit 4: Family & Relationships • Units 1–3 cumulative review

Set up your duo

Use one device. Only the signer looks at the secret prompt; the guesser looks away. Switch roles every challenge. No sign-recognition software is used: partners award points together.

Guesser: look away. Signer: reveal the secret card when ready.

Power-ups: each player gets one Double XP, one Hint, and one Reroll for the whole game. Reroll swaps the card within this round. Hint displays a category or skill clue. Both are optional.

Score this turn together

Award points only after the signer performs and the partner attempts an interpretation. Signer and guesser should agree on each check.

Earn your first successful turn to unlock badges.
